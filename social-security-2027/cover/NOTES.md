# Cover notes: Social Security, Claimed On Purpose

## Series position
Book Two of the **Plain Talk Retirement Series** (Walter Brennan). The cover deliberately reuses the Book One (`medicare-2027`) grammar so the two read as one shelf, changing only the accent and the motif:

| | Book One: Medicare Made Plain | Book Two: this book |
|---|---|---|
| Ground | ivory `#f4eee2` | ivory `#f4eee2` (same) |
| Structure | accent hairline top, navy slab bottom | identical |
| Accent | brick red `#b1392b` | pine green `#0e6a58` |
| Title | Georgia caps + knockout accent bar | identical |
| Motif | A-B-C-D descending staircase | 62 / 67 / 70 ascending columns |

The ascending columns are the deliberate inverse of Book One's descending steps: Medicare is a sequence you walk down in order; Social Security is a number that grows the longer you wait. Same drawing language, opposite direction.

## Concepts considered
1. **Ivory + pine, 62/67/70 ascending columns (chosen)** — light ground, pine hairline and knockout title bar, three lettered columns stepping up with the claiming ages set in knockout serif, navy footer slab carrying subtitle, tagline and author. The whole claiming argument is legible as a shape before a single word is read.
2. **Navy + gold Social Security card** — instantly on-genre, but the card is the single most copied object in this category, it edges toward a look-alike cover, and card-with-numbers turns to mush at 160 px.
3. **Cream + teal fork-in-the-road** — the "big decision" metaphor. Too generic, says nothing about Social Security specifically, and thin path strokes vanish in a thumbnail.

## Title treatment
Four title lines, at the cap: "SOCIAL" and "SECURITY," in Georgia caps at 215 px (8.4% of cover height each) in navy on ivory, then "CLAIMED" / "ON PURPOSE" at 170 px knocked out in cream on a full pine bar. The bar is the strongest shape on the cover and it carries the promise. Cover subtitle is the 5-word "The 2027 Decision Guide" with the remainder of `book.json`'s long subtitle set as the tagline below; title, subtitle wording and author (Walter Brennan) all match `book.json` exactly. Light ground, so the whole cover carries the 4 px medium-gray `#9aa2a6` KDP border.

## Competitor conventions in this niche
Social Security covers cluster on navy or red-white-blue grounds with a card graphic, a nest egg, a piggy bank, or a smiling couple on a dock, usually with a gold or white sans headline and a long small-type subtitle crammed at the bottom. Points of difference here: a *light* ivory ground, pine green rather than patriotic blue or gold, a knockout serif bar instead of a sans headline, an abstract age-column motif instead of a card, and all support text grouped into one dark slab so it reads as a single block rather than scattered lines.
